Yes, for most of your practice. Old 0450 and 7115 questions still test topics in the 2027 syllabus, and I could match every part of a 2025 paper to a 0264 section. Three things changed: Paper 1 questions have four parts, not five; the long Paper 1 answer in the specimen is worth 8 marks and rewards application; and the syllabus says every Paper 2 part (a) is now applied.

There are no 0264 or 7081 past papers yet, because the new syllabuses are for exams in 2027, 2028 and 2029. So old papers plus the specimens are what you have. This article is for students and is only about practice papers. If you want the full picture of what changed in the course, read my [guide to the 0264 and 7081 changes for parents](/blog/igcse-business-0264-o-level-7081-changes-2027-parents).

## What still matches between the old papers and the 2027 exams?

I compared the June 2025 papers 0450/12 and 0450/22 with the 2027 syllabus and the 0264 specimen papers. The O Level paper 7115/12 from the same session has exactly the same questions as 0450/12, so everything here applies to O Level too. I also checked the marks on every old Paper 1 and Paper 2 on disk from 2022 to 2026 (51 of each across both codes): all of them use the mark pattern in the table.

| Feature | 0450 and 7115 (June 2025 papers) | 0264 and 7081 (syllabus and specimens) |
|---|---|---|
| Paper 1 | Short Answer and Data Response, 1 hour 30 minutes, 80 marks | Same name, same time, same marks |
| Paper 1 questions | Four, each in five parts: 2, 2, 4, 6 and 6 marks | Four, each in four parts: 2, 4, 6 and 8 marks |
| Paper 2 | Case Study with an insert, four questions, 1 hour 30 minutes, 80 marks | Same |
| Paper 2 parts | Each question has an 8 mark part (a) and a 12 mark part (b) | Same split |
| 12 mark levels | Level 3 is 9 to 12, Level 2 is 5 to 8, Level 1 is 1 to 4 | Same bands |

Four things carry straight over.

- **The short Paper 1 parts look the same.** A 2 mark definition, a 4 mark "outline" part with a mark for each point and a mark for linking it to the business, and a 6 mark "explain two" part with knowledge, application and analysis marks. The specimen marks these the same way the June 2025 scheme does.
- **The Paper 2 level tables look the same.** The 2027 syllabus says the Paper 2 mark scheme has been reworded, but that overall it marks in much the same way. Both schemes reward detailed discussion of the options and a recommendation that explains why the others were rejected.
- **Old Paper 2 already mixes two and three options.** The June 2025 Paper 22 used two options in each part (b), and Paper 21 from the same session used three in three of its parts. The specimen uses three in two of its four.
- **The topics map across.** My [0450 past papers by topic](/past-papers/igcse-business-studies-0450/topics) sort all 256 tagged 0450 questions from 2022 to 2026 under the 0264 topic list. That count is whole questions (every part of a Paper 1 question, or both parts of a Paper 2 question), each filed under a main topic and usually a second one.

## What is different on the new Paper 1?

The 2027 syllabus lists this change: each Paper 1 question drops from five parts to four, with a new split of marks. Here is how the old parts line up with the new ones, matched by marks and question type.

| Old part (2025) | Typical task | Nearest specimen part |
|---|---|---|
| (a) 2 marks | Define a term | (a) 2 marks, same task |
| (b) 2 marks | Identify two, or a short calculation | (a) 2 marks, or a 2 mark half of (b) |
| (c) 4 marks | Outline two points linked to the business | (b) 4 marks, same task |
| (d) 6 marks | Explain two points with knowledge, application and analysis | (c) 6 marks, same task |
| (e) 6 marks | Justify a decision | (d) 8 marks, now with application |

The last row is the one that matters. In the June 2025 Paper 12 mark scheme, every part (e) carries the same note: "This is a general question so there are no marks for application." The marks were 2 for points, 2 for development and 2 for a justified decision.

In the 0264 specimen, every part (d) gives up to 2 marks each for knowledge, application, analysis and a justified decision. So the long answer grew from 6 to 8 marks, and the 2 new marks are for using the business in the question.

This changes a habit. The November 2023 report for 7115 Paper 12 told candidates that application written into a part (e) answer could not gain credit. On the specimen's marking, leaving it out of part (d) costs you up to 2 marks.

## What is different on the new Paper 2?

The 2027 syllabus says: "All part (a) questions are now applied questions." Old papers were allowed one exception. The June 2025 report for 0450 Paper 22 tells candidates to expect one part (a) that is normally not tied to the case.

You can see it in that paper. Question 3(a) asked for four ways market research helps any business, and the mark scheme gave no application marks for it. In the specimen, parts 1(a), 2(a) and 4(a) all need the cafe business in the case, and 3(a) starts with calculations from the case's own cost figures. Only its last small part, listing four uses of cost data for any business, is general.

## Do the old papers still match the 2027 marks and wording?

The skills are the same four: knowledge, application, analysis and evaluation. The syllabus says the objectives have new wording but still assess what the old ones did. It also says application now carries more weight and the other three carry less.

| Skill | Paper 1 (2027) | Paper 2 (2027) | Whole qualification |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Knowledge and understanding | 40% | 30% | 35% |
| Application | 30% | 30% | 30% |
| Analysis | 20% | 25% | 22% |
| Evaluation | 10% | 15% | 13% |

Watch one name as well. The June 2025 Paper 12 shows an extract from an "income statement". The 2027 syllabus calls it the statement of profit or loss. It is the same document. My advice: learn both names and use the new one in your answers.

## Which 2027 topics do old papers barely cover?

The syllabus "changes" page names two new topics: 4.2 Technology and production of goods and services, and 4.3 Sustainable production of goods and services. It also says digital methods of communication were added to topic 2.

On my topic pages, only 9 of the 256 tagged 0450 questions carry 4.2 or 4.3 as their main or second topic. For 7115 it is 4 of 152 whole questions. A few old questions do come close, and they are worth doing:

- **November 2025 Paper 11, Question 1(d):** effects of a furniture maker bringing in new technology.
- **June 2025 Paper 22, Question 2(b):** choosing between more automation and better labour skills.
- **November 2025 Paper 22, Question 4(b):** two ways a business could become more sustainable, one of them renewable electricity.

Each one appears in both 0450 and 7115 for that session.

I also searched all 102 old question papers on disk (0450 and 7115, 2022 to 2026) for some terms the 2027 syllabus lists. None of them mentions dynamic pricing, contactless payments or 3D printing, and none asks about employment contracts. The specimen asks about two of these: dynamic pricing in Paper 1 and employment contracts in Paper 2. For these, practise from the [0264 syllabus checklist](/syllabus/igcse-business-0264) or the [7081 syllabus checklist](/syllabus/o-level-business-7081) and the specimens.

The changes page also says content has been taken out, but it does not list what. I have no old syllabus on disk to compare, so I cannot tell you which old questions are now off the syllabus. My advice: if an old question tests something you cannot find in the 2027 checklist, check the official syllabus before spending time on it. The 2027 syllabus also says you will not be asked to construct a full cash flow forecast, statement of profit or loss or statement of financial position, or to do exchange rate calculations.

## How does one 2025 paper map onto the 2027 syllabus?

Here is the June 2025 Paper 12 (0450/12, the same as 7115/12), part by part. The topic labels are my own reading of the 2027 syllabus, not Cambridge's. Keep the paper open: I describe each question in my own words.

| Question | Business | Parts and marks | 2027 sections tested |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A shoe maker using batch production | 2, 2, 4, 6, 6 | 4.1, 3.3, 4.5, 3.1, 3.3 |
| 2 | A furniture retailer with hundreds of shops | 2, 2, 4, 6, 6 | 5.3, 5.3, 5.3, 2.1, 2.4 |
| 3 | A bakery that one man plans to open | 2, 2, 4, 6, 6 | 1.2, 1.3, 1.5, 1.3, 5.1 |
| 4 | A tyre maker with factories in six countries | 2, 2, 4, 6, 6 | 4.4, 4.4, 6.2, 6.2, 2.3 |

All 20 parts land in a 2027 section: 14 different sections across all six topics. Nothing on this paper is wasted practice.

Notice two things. First, each question jumps between topics, just as the specimen does, so revising one chapter at a time is not enough. Second, my topic pages list a whole question under two topics, but a question can test four. Question 1 touches production, marketing and quality.

### How would Question 3 look in the 2027 shape?

The 20 marks are the same. Only the split changes. This is how I teach students to turn an old question into new style practice.

| 2027 part | Use this old part | What to change |
|---|---|---|
| (a) 2 marks | 3(a), define a term | Nothing |
| (b) 4 marks | 3(c), identify four stakeholder groups | Nothing (the specimen has an "identify four" part worth 4) |
| (c) 6 marks | 3(d), explain two characteristics of an entrepreneur | Nothing |
| (d) 8 marks | 3(e), is a bank loan the best source of capital | Add a fact from the stem to each point |

Old 3(b), worth 2 marks, has no slot. Do it as a quick extra.

For part (d), the old question asked about any new business, so its scheme gave no marks for the bakery facts. Answer it as if it named the owner, because every part (d) in the specimen names its business. My model, tagged the way the specimen tags a part (d):

- **For a loan:** a loan gives a large sum at once [k], enough to equip the new bakery before it opens [app], so the owner does not have to wait years to save it [an].
- **Against a loan:** interest and repayments are due every month [k], and the owner is leaving his job, so he will have no salary to fall back on while the bakery finds customers [app]. Cash outflows could be larger than inflows in the first months [an].
- **Decision:** a loan is not the best choice for the whole amount [eval]. A business with no trading record may be refused or charged more, so putting in his own savings first and borrowing less keeps the monthly payments small enough to survive a slow start [eval].

That is 2 marks each for knowledge, application, analysis and evaluation, which is 8. Mark your old answer with the old scheme, then give yourself up to 2 more for application only if each point uses a different fact from the stem.

## Where do students lose marks on old papers that still matter in 2027?

These rows come from the 0450 and 7115 examiner reports. Each skill is still tested in the specimen.

| Mistake | What the report said (my summary) | Report |
|---|---|---|
| Using the same fact from the stem for both points | Of the candidates who applied their answers, most used one reference twice; each point needs its own | 0450 June 2025, Paper 12 |
| Writing "the product" instead of naming it | Some candidates stated "product" instead of the real product or service | 7115 November 2023, Paper 11 |
| The same analysis under two points | Repeat a line of analysis for the second point and it scores one time only | 0450 June 2025, Paper 12 |
| Adding new points instead of developing one | In 1(e), many gave new knowledge points where analysis was needed | 0450 June 2025, Paper 12 |
| No decision, or a decision that only sums up | Many part (e) answers had no decision at all; most that did gave no reasoned support | 7115 June 2023, Paper 12 |
| Profit margin in dollars or without % | Candidates often dropped the × 100 or the % sign, and others answered in dollars | 0450 March 2025, Paper 12, 3(b) |
| No figures when the question asks for calculations | Weaker answers only compared appendix figures, and some said an option lost money without giving the amount | 0450 June 2023, Paper 22, 2(b) |
| A long list in a 12 mark part | A list of points usually scores in Level 1 only; explaining a point can lift it to Level 2 | 0450 June 2025, Paper 22 |

The June 2025 report for 0450 Paper 12 put the decision rule plainly: "A decision alone or summary of earlier points is not evaluation." The specimen mark scheme makes a similar point for every part (d): a justification that only goes over what you already wrote does not count. For a full method on the decision, see my guide to [justify and evaluate questions](/blog/igcse-business-0450-justify-evaluate-questions).

## How should I practise with old papers and the specimens?

There is one specimen of each paper and dozens of old papers. This is how I teach it.

1. **Learn by topic first.** Pick a 2027 section on the topic pages above and do every old question on it.
2. **Do whole old papers next.** Download them from the [0450 past papers page](/past-papers/igcse-business-studies-0450) or the 7115 page next to it. Paper 1 still runs 90 minutes for 80 marks, so the timing practice is real.
3. **Upgrade every old part (e).** Write it as an 8 mark part (d): two points, each with a different fact from the stem, then a decision with a reason you have not used yet.
4. **Upgrade the generic Paper 2 part (a).** Answer it, then add one fact from the case to every point.
5. **Fill the gaps with new material.** For 4.2, 4.3 and terms like dynamic pricing, use the three old questions above, the specimens and the practice questions below.
6. **Keep the specimens for a full timed mock** once you have covered all six topics. My [0264 and 7081 specimen paper walkthrough](/blog/0264-7081-business-specimen-paper-2027) explains every question.

For O Level students: the 7081 Paper 1 specimen question paper is not on disk here, so I have not checked it. The 7081 Paper 1 mark scheme and Paper 2 question paper I have are word for word the same as the 0264 versions apart from the code and qualification name. Use the 0264 Paper 1 specimen if you cannot find the 7081 one.

<Tip title="Count your stem facts before you write part (d)">
Before an 8 mark part (d), underline two different facts in the stem: a number, the product, the type of business, a plan. Use one in each point. On the old papers that habit earned nothing in part (e). On the specimen it is worth up to 2 marks a question.
</Tip>

The [0450 grade thresholds](/grade-thresholds/igcse-business-0450) show the marks old grades needed. No thresholds exist for 0264 or 7081 yet, so treat them as background only.

## Can you answer three questions in the 2027 style?

These are my own questions, with my own businesses and figures.

**1. Calculate (2 + 2 marks).** Zara makes leather school bags in a small workshop. Her fixed costs are $9100 a month. Each bag sells for $18 and has a variable cost of $11. She expects to sell 1750 bags a month.

(i) Calculate the break even output. Show your working. [2]
(ii) Calculate the margin of safety. Show your working. [2]

*Answer:*
- (i) Contribution per bag = $18 minus $11 = $7. Break even output = fixed costs ÷ contribution per unit = 9100 ÷ 7 = **1300 bags**.
- (ii) Margin of safety = actual sales minus break even sales = 1750 minus 1300 = **450 bags**.

Both answers are in bags, not dollars. Following the specimen pattern, a correct answer with no working would earn both marks, and a correct method with a slip would earn 1.

**2. Outline (4 marks).** RXP runs 14 pharmacies. Queues are long at busy times, and staff spend the last half hour of each day counting cash. The owner plans to accept contactless payments. Outline two ways contactless payments could improve productivity at RXP.

*Answer:*
- Payments are quicker [k], so the long queues at RXP's busy times move faster and each employee serves more customers an hour [app].
- Less cash is handled [k], so staff no longer lose the last half hour of the day counting it and can restock shelves instead [app].

Four tags for four marks: knowledge and application for each way, the same split as the specimen's 4 mark parts.

**3. Justify (8 marks).** STU makes school uniforms in a factory with 220 employees. It pays a waste company to collect about 2 tonnes of fabric offcuts every month. The directors plan to buy sewing machines and turn the offcuts into small drawstring bags to sell to the schools it already supplies. Do you think the advantages for STU of reusing its fabric waste are greater than the disadvantages? Justify your answer.

*Answer:*
- **Advantage:** the business uses fewer resources and creates less waste [k]. STU would no longer pay to have 2 tonnes of offcuts collected each month [app], so its costs fall and profit could rise [an].
- **Disadvantage:** new equipment is needed [k], here extra sewing machines for the offcuts [app]. That is a large cash outflow before a single bag is sold [an].
- **Decision:** the advantages are greater [eval], because the schools already buy from STU, so it has ready customers for the bags and needs little new marketing. This holds only if each bag sells for more than it costs to make [eval].

That is 2 tags each for knowledge, application, analysis and evaluation, matching the specimen's 8 mark part (d). The decision uses a reason that is not in the two points above it.

## FAQ

### Can I use 0450 past papers for 0264?

Yes. I mapped every part of the June 2025 Paper 12 onto a 2027 section. Upgrade every old part (e) to the 8 mark style with application, and add extra practice for 4.2 and 4.3, which very few old questions test.

### Are there any 0264 past papers yet?

No. The 0264 and 7081 syllabuses are for exams in 2027, 2028 and 2029, so the only new style papers are Cambridge's specimens. Old 0450 and 7115 papers are the best extra practice.

### Can O Level 7081 students use 0450 papers as well as 7115?

Yes. The 0264 and 7081 syllabuses list the same topics, and the June 2025 Paper 12 for 0450 and 7115 had identical questions. Use both to get more practice.

### Is Paper 2 very different from the old Case Study paper?

Not much. The time, marks, question layout and 12 mark level bands match. The main change is that the syllabus now calls every part (a) an applied question, while old papers usually had one generic part (a).

### What should I skip in old papers?

The 2027 syllabus says some content was removed but does not list it. If an old question tests something missing from the 2027 checklist, check the official syllabus before practising it. Do not skip questions just because they use the older name "income statement".

### Will the 2027 papers look like the specimen?

The syllabus tells teachers to use the specimens alongside it to see the new question styles. They show the format, not which topics will come up, so cover every section of the syllabus.
