The 7707 and 0452 specimen papers for 2027 are Cambridge's official samples of the new exams, and both codes use exactly the same questions. Paper 1 has 40 multiple choice questions in 1 hour 30 minutes. Paper 2 keeps five compulsory questions of 20 marks each. The new ethics and technology topics appear in both papers: three questions in Paper 1 and 13 marks in Paper 2.

I describe every question in my own words and do not reproduce Cambridge's questions or figures, so keep the papers open as you read.

## Where can I find the 7707 and 0452 specimen papers?

Download them from Cambridge. Go to the official [Cambridge O Level Accounting 7707 page](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/cambridge-o-level-accounting-7707/) or the [Cambridge IGCSE Accounting 0452 page](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/cambridge-igcse-accounting-0452/) and look for the specimen papers for examination from 2027.

There are four documents for each code:

| Document | What it contains |
|---|---|
| Paper 1 specimen paper | 40 multiple choice questions |
| Paper 1 specimen mark scheme | The correct letter for each question |
| Paper 2 specimen paper | Five structured questions, answered on the paper |
| Paper 2 specimen mark scheme | Model answers, mark by mark, with marking rules |

Each one is marked for examination from 2027, and the 2027 to 2029 syllabus tells teachers to use these updated specimen materials alongside it. For the full list of syllabus changes, read my [7707 syllabus 2027 article](/blog/o-level-accounting-7707-syllabus-2027) or the [0452 syllabus 2027 article](/blog/igcse-accounting-0452-syllabus-2027-changes).

## Are the 7707 and 0452 specimen papers the same?

Yes. I compared the text of all four 7707 documents with the four 0452 documents line by line. The only differences are the qualification name on the cover and the paper code printed on each page. Every question, every figure and every mark scheme answer is identical.

So an O Level student can use the 0452 specimen and an IGCSE student can use the 7707 one. Either way you get one Paper 1 and one Paper 2, not two different sets.

## What does the 2027 Paper 1 specimen look like?

Paper 1 is the paper that changed. The specimen confirms the new format:

| Feature | 2027 specimen |
|---|---|
| Questions | 40, all compulsory |
| Options per question | Four (A, B, C, D) |
| Marks | 1 per question, 40 in total |
| Time | 1 hour 30 minutes |
| Calculator | Allowed |
| Weighting (from the syllabus) | 30% of the qualification |

That is 2 minutes 15 seconds per question. Each correct answer scores one mark and nothing is taken off for a wrong one, so never leave a question blank.

### Which topics does Paper 1 test, and how often?

I sorted every question into the seven syllabus topics. This is my own count, because Cambridge does not label the questions.

| Syllabus topic | Questions | Number |
|---|---|---:|
| 1 The fundamentals of accounting | 1, 2 | 2 |
| 2 Sources and recording of data | 3, 5, 6, 7 | 4 |
| 3 Verification of accounting records | 8 to 14 | 7 |
| 4 Accounting procedures | 4, 15 to 20 | 7 |
| 5 Preparation of financial statements | 21, 23 to 30 | 9 |
| 6 Analysis and interpretation | 22, 31 to 36 | 7 |
| 7 Accounting concepts and modern practice | 37 to 40 | 4 |
| **Total** | | **40** |

Three things stand out.

- **Every topic is tested.** Topic 5 has the most questions, covering sole traders, partnerships, a limited company, a manufacturing cost, a club and incomplete records.
- **The questions run mostly in syllabus order.** Topic 7, including the new content, comes at the very end. If you run short of time, the questions you skip are the new ones, so watch the clock.
- **About a third need a calculation.** By my count, 14 of the 40 questions need working before you can choose an answer: a trade discount, a control account balance, a disposal, an allowance for irrecoverable debts, inventory valuation, drawings, a partnership share, equity, club funds, incomplete records and ratios.

### Which Paper 1 questions test the 2027 changes?

Three questions test the two new topics directly:

- **Question 38 (7.2 Ethics):** pick the description that matches one of the five fundamental principles.
- **Question 39 (7.3 Technology and sustainability):** decide which of four habits make data storage sustainable.
- **Question 40 (7.3):** spot the false statement about accounting software, manual records and digital documents.

The technology theme reaches older topics too. Question 37 tests an accounting concept (7.1) through a software example, and Questions 1, 5 and 24 use software as the setting for the role of an accountant, the three column running balance format and the benefits of a new partner.

The updated ratio list shows up in the Topic 6 questions. One asks for capital employed from a list of balances. Another asks for return on capital employed for a company with debentures, so you need the formula in the 2027 list: profit for the year before interest, over capital employed. Others work backwards from a profit margin and a rate of inventory turnover to a missing figure, or ask what explains a fall in the acid test ratio or a gap between two businesses' profit margins.

<Tip title="Work it out before you read the options">
For every calculation question, cover the four options and do the working on the question paper first. Then find your answer among the options. Wrong options are often the figure you get from one common slip, such as forgetting a return or using the wrong side of a balance. My [Paper 1 multiple choice method](/blog/igcse-o-level-accounting-paper-1-mcq-tips) shows this step by step.
</Tip>

The new topics are only three questions. The 14 calculation questions carry far more marks, and a calculation done in a hurry lands on a wrong option. If that keeps happening to you, it is a gap in method, not in speed.

## What is in Paper 2 of the 2027 specimen, question by question?

The syllabus makes no changes to Paper 2 for 2027: five compulsory questions, 20 marks each, 100 marks in 1 hour 45 minutes, answered on the question paper. The front cover asks you to show your workings and to use international accounting terms and formats.

| Question | Business and main topics | Marks |
|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Retail sole trader: purchases journal, supplier accounts, computerised accounting | 20 |
| 2 | Accountant checking a client's books: errors, suspense account, corrected profit, concepts, ethics | 20 |
| 3 | Manufacturer: manufacturing account, trading section, choosing a supplier | 20 |
| 4 | Social club: subscriptions, income and expenditure, accumulated fund, secure records | 20 |
| 5 | Limited company: company or partnership, changes in equity, payables and receivables days, credit control | 20 |

Here is each question part by part. The marks are Cambridge's; the descriptions are mine.

### Question 1: a retailer's purchases and a computer decision

- **(a) 3 marks.** Give three reasons a trader needs to work out profit or loss.
- **(b) 4 marks.** Write up a purchases journal from three supplier invoices, two of them with trade discount. Total it and name the account the total is posted to.
- **(c) 1 mark.** Suggest why a supplier's account started the month with a debit balance.
- **(d) 7 marks.** Write up and balance two supplier accounts for a month, with a return, a bank payment, and a settlement that earns cash discount.
- **(e) 5 marks.** Advise the owner whether to switch to a computerised accounting system, with arguments for and against.

**What is new for 2027:** part (e) tests 7.3, the use of digital applications for accounting records. Part (a) is Topic 1 and parts (b) to (d) are familiar Topic 2 work. Remember that trade discount comes off before anything is recorded, while cash discount is recorded when the debt is settled.

### Question 2: errors, concepts and ethics

- **(a) and (b) 2 marks each.** Define two types of error. Each definition earns one mark for the basic idea and one for the detail that separates it from the other type.
- **(c) 4 marks.** Prepare a suspense account from a list of errors, with the original trial balance difference as the balancing figure.
- **(d) 6 marks.** Complete a table showing how correcting each error changes the draft profit (increase, decrease or no effect), then work out the corrected profit.
- **(e) 2 marks.** Name the accounting concept applied when correcting two of the errors.
- **(f) 2 marks.** Name the fundamental ethical principle shown in two short situations.
- **(g) 2 marks.** Explain why an accountant should follow the ethical framework.

**What is new for 2027:** parts (f) and (g) test 7.2, worth 4 marks. In this specimen, ethics is a short add on to a familiar errors question, not a separate essay.

### Question 3: a manufacturing business

- **(a) 1 mark.** State what work in progress means.
- **(b) 11 marks.** Prepare a manufacturing account. The adjustments include reducing balance depreciation on factory machinery, an insurance prepayment, rent and insurance split between factory and office, and accrued indirect wages.
- **(c) 3 marks.** Prepare the trading section of the statement of profit or loss using the cost of production.
- **(d) 5 marks.** Advise whether to change to a new raw materials supplier offering a bigger trade discount and more frequent deliveries, with two arguments each way.

**What is new for 2027:** nothing in the content. Part (b) is the biggest single part on the paper. My [manufacturing accounts guide](/blog/igcse-o-level-accounting-manufacturing-accounts) covers the layout.

### Question 4: a social club

- **(a) 7 marks.** Prepare and balance a subscriptions account with arrears and advance payments at both ends of the year, plus refunds.
- **(b) 7 marks.** Prepare an income and expenditure account. You need the net result of social events, a prepayment, an accrual, a loss on disposal and depreciation.
- **(c) 2 marks.** Calculate the closing accumulated fund.
- **(d) 4 marks.** State four ways to keep computerised records secure.

**What is new for 2027:** part (d) tests 7.3, the safe storage of accounting data, worth 4 marks. The club result in the specimen is a deficit, so read the sign of your answer before you adjust the accumulated fund.

### Question 5: a limited company and its credit control

- **(a) 2 marks and (b) 1 mark.** Give advantages and a disadvantage of a limited company compared with a partnership.
- **(c) 5 marks.** Prepare a statement of changes in equity with profit, an interim dividend given per share, and a transfer to general reserve. You must first work out the number of shares from the share capital and the nominal value.
- **(d) 4 marks.** Calculate trade payables turnover and trade receivables turnover in days, rounded up to the next whole day as the question instructs.
- **(e) 3 marks.** Explain why the directors should be worried when this year's ratios are compared with last year's.
- **(f) 5 marks.** Advise the directors on two credit control options, with an advantage and a disadvantage of each.

**What is new for 2027:** nothing in the content. Parts (d) and (e) use two ratios that stay on the updated list. Only part of the revenue is on credit, and the mark scheme works out receivables days on credit sales only. Read every line of the question before you choose your figures.

## How does the specimen test the new 2027 topics?

Here is where the new and updated content sits, across both papers:

| Area | Paper 1 | Paper 2 |
|---|---|---|
| 7.1 Accounting concepts | 1 question | 2 marks (Q2e) |
| 7.2 Ethical considerations | 1 question | 4 marks (Q2f, Q2g) |
| 7.3 Technology and sustainability | 2 questions | 9 marks (Q1e, Q4d) |
| Topic 6 ratios and interpretation | 7 questions | 7 marks (Q5d, Q5e) |

### How is ethics tested?

Both papers describe a principle or a situation and expect you to name or match it. For the "why it matters" part, the mark scheme rewards general points about trust in accountants, better decisions by stakeholders and the reputation of the profession.

The five principles are integrity, objectivity, professional competence and due care, confidentiality, and professional behaviour. Learn each with a one line situation of your own. A list of names alone will not be enough.

### How is technology and sustainability tested?

The specimen asks practical questions, not technical ones, and the mark scheme rewards ordinary, sensible points such as backups, passwords, malware protection, training costs and the risk of data loss.

The syllabus says you do not need to know any specific accounting software. Prepare a short list of advantages, disadvantages and security steps, and practise turning them into a judgement.

### How are the updated ratios tested?

The syllabus says its formulas are the only ones accepted, and the mark scheme follows them exactly. Learn all ten formulas word for word from my [accounting ratios guide](/blog/igcse-o-level-accounting-ratios-formulas), and always write the formula, the figures and the answer with its unit.

## What does the mark scheme reward?

The Paper 1 mark scheme is only a list of correct letters. The Paper 2 mark scheme is far more useful, because it shows where each mark sits.

- **Figures earn marks one by one.** Most marks are for a correct figure with the correct label, on the correct side. A neat layout helps the examiner find them.
- **The own figure rule protects you.** If you make an error early, later marks can still be given when your method is correct and complete. So never stop because one figure looks wrong.
- **Workings matter.** A correct answer usually earns full marks without working, but if your answer is wrong, a clear working can still earn the method marks.
- **Different wording is accepted.** For written parts, the mark scheme ends its lists of points with "Accept all valid responses." Any correct point in your own words counts.
- **Decisions need both sides.** In the "advise" parts of Questions 1 and 3, up to 4 marks go to arguments for and against (no more than 3 on one side), and 1 mark goes to a recommendation backed by at least one point on each side.

Question 5(f) is marked differently: one mark for an advantage and one for a disadvantage of each option, then one for the recommendation.

Questions 1(e), 3(d) and 5(f) are worth 5 marks each: 15 marks of judgement. That is in line with the 15% of Paper 2 the syllabus gives to evaluation.

### What does a full mark "advise" answer look like?

Here is my own example, not from the specimen. A tutor keeps her records in paper files and is thinking of moving them to a cloud service. Advise her.

1. **For:** the records can be reached from any device, so she can update them on the same day.
2. **For:** the service keeps backup copies, so a fire or a lost file does not destroy her records.
3. **Against:** she pays a monthly fee, which reduces her profit.
4. **Against:** if her password is weak, someone could access her student and payment records.
5. **Decision:** she should move to the cloud service, because protection against losing her records is worth the fee, as long as she uses a strong password.

Four separate points, two each side, then a clear decision that refers back to them. That structure fits Questions 1(e) and 3(d). For 5(f), give one advantage and one disadvantage of each option, then decide.

<Tip title="Put your decision in a sentence of its own">
Write the recommendation as a separate final sentence that starts with "She should" or "The directors should". A decision hidden inside an argument is easy for you to forget and easy for an examiner to miss.
</Tip>

## How should I use the specimen paper in revision?

There is only one specimen of each paper, so save it for a full mock once you have covered all seven topics.

1. **Sit Paper 1 in 90 minutes** with a calculator and nothing else. Aim to finish the first pass in about 75 minutes, then use the rest on questions you marked to check.
2. **Sit Paper 2 in 105 minutes**, on the same day or the next. Allow about a minute per mark, so roughly 21 minutes per question.
3. **Mark Paper 1** with the letter key. Use my topic table above to group your wrong answers by topic.
4. **Mark Paper 2 strictly** with the mark scheme. Tick each figure mark separately and apply the own figure rule fairly.
5. **Write down your marks per question**, not just the total. The lowest question tells you which topic to fix first.
6. **Fix that topic with past papers.** Use the [7707 past papers](/past-papers/o-level-accounting-7707) and the list of [most repeated 7707 topics](/past-papers/o-level-accounting-7707/most-repeated-topics) to find more questions on it.
7. **Practise Paper 1 in sets of 40 questions in 90 minutes**, built from past paper questions, so the new length feels normal.

Past papers will not cover 7.2 and 7.3, because those topics are new. For those, use the specimen questions and the [7707 syllabus checklist](/syllabus/o-level-accounting-7707), and write your own short practice answers for each point.

## FAQ

### Where can I find the 7707 specimen paper 2027?

On Cambridge's official O Level Accounting 7707 page, in the specimen papers for examination from 2027. The same papers appear on the IGCSE Accounting 0452 page under that code.

### Is the 0452 specimen paper the same as the 7707 one?

Yes. Apart from the qualification name and paper code, the 0452 and 7707 specimen papers and mark schemes are identical.

### Is the specimen paper harder than past papers?

There is no official answer, because the specimen comes with no grade thresholds. The syllabus makes no changes to Paper 2. Paper 1 is five questions longer with 15 more minutes, and the new topics are a small share: three questions in Paper 1 and 13 marks in Paper 2.

### How many questions are in the 2027 Paper 1 specimen?

40 multiple choice questions, one mark each, in 1 hour 30 minutes. In 2026 and earlier, Paper 1 had 35 questions in 1 hour 15 minutes.

### Do I need to learn a particular accounting software for 2027?

No. The syllabus says you do not need to know any specific accounting software. The specimen asks about the general advantages, disadvantages and safe use of digital records.

### Are past papers still useful for the 2027 exam?

Yes. Paper 2 has not changed, so every past Paper 2 is good practice. Past Paper 1 questions still cover Topics 1 to 6, but past papers do not test the new 7.2 and 7.3 content, so use the specimen for those.
