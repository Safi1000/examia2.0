Cambridge IGCSE and Cambridge O Level are two separate qualifications from the same board, Cambridge International, and Cambridge says they are at the same level. In Accounting, Economics and Business, the 2027 papers and topic lists are identical. Four things differ: the code, the grade scale (IGCSE runs A\* to G, O Level A\* to E), the exam series, and where O Level is offered.

I checked this by comparing the six Cambridge syllabuses for 2027 to 2029 in pairs. That is 0452 with 7707, 0455 with 2281, and 0264 with 7081.

## Are IGCSE and O Level the same qualification?

No. They are two qualifications with separate codes and certificates. But neither is the "higher" one. Each of the six syllabuses states that IGCSE, IGCSE (9-1) and O Level syllabuses are "at the same level" (wording from the 0452 syllabus).

Cambridge describes them a little differently. The IGCSE syllabuses call IGCSE the most popular international qualification in the world for 14 to 16 year olds. The O Level syllabuses say O Level is designed for learners whose first language may not be English. Neither line changes what you study or how you are marked.

### What actually differs between IGCSE and O Level?

| | Cambridge IGCSE | Cambridge O Level |
|---|---|---|
| Grades | A\* to G | A\* to E |
| Exam series | June and November, plus March for schools in India | June and November |
| Where it is offered | No zone limit stated in these three syllabuses | Not available in all administrative zones |
| Codes in my subjects | 0452, 0455, 0264 | 7707, 2281, 7081 |

Cambridge puts every school in one of six administrative zones, based on where the school is. Each zone has its own exam timetable.

## What are the IGCSE and O Level codes for Accounting, Economics and Business?

| Subject | IGCSE | O Level |
|---|---|---|
| Accounting | 0452 | 7707 |
| Economics | 0455 | 2281 |
| Business (exams up to 2026) | Business Studies 0450 | Business Studies 7115 |
| Business (exams from 2027) | Business 0264 | Business 7081 |

Business is the one that confuses families. From 2027 the subject is called Business, not Business Studies, and both codes are new. If a school letter still shows 0450 or 7115 for a 2027 exam, ask about it.

## Is IGCSE Accounting 0452 the same as O Level Accounting 7707?

In papers and content, yes.

| 2027 to 2029 | 0452 and 7707 (identical) |
|---|---|
| Paper 1 Multiple Choice | 40 questions, 40 marks, 1 hour 30 minutes, 30% |
| Paper 2 Structured Written Paper | Five compulsory questions, 100 marks, 1 hour 45 minutes, 70% |

I compared the subject content pages word for word. The seven topics, every subtopic and every ratio formula match. The only difference is the order of a few labels in the ratio table.

The 7707 syllabus confirms it. It says it shares content with other accounting syllabuses, and that textbooks endorsed for IGCSE Accounting are suitable for it.

The 2027 changes are also the same for both codes. Paper 1 grows from 35 to 40 questions, and topic 7 gains ethics, technology and sustainability. I cover them in my articles on [IGCSE Accounting 0452 for 2027](/blog/igcse-accounting-0452-syllabus-2027-changes) and [O Level Accounting 7707 for 2027](/blog/o-level-accounting-7707-syllabus-2027).

## Is IGCSE Economics 0455 the same as O Level Economics 2281?

Yes, again.

| 2027 to 2029 | 0455 and 2281 (identical) |
|---|---|
| Paper 1 Multiple Choice | 40 questions, 40 marks, 1 hour, 30% |
| Paper 2 Structured Questions | 80 marks, 2 hours, 70%. Section A: one compulsory question. Section B: three questions from four |

The six topics and every numbered point match word for word. The 2281 syllabus also says textbooks endorsed for IGCSE Economics are suitable for it.

One caution. Cambridge has published a separate syllabus update for 2281. I compared the main syllabus documents, so if your teacher mentions the update, check it against your notes.

Both codes change in the same way for 2027. Paper 2 drops from 90 marks to 80, and from 2 hours 15 minutes to 2 hours. The full lists are in my articles on [IGCSE Economics 0455 for 2027](/blog/igcse-economics-0455-syllabus-2027-changes) and [O Level Economics 2281 for 2027](/blog/o-level-economics-2281-syllabus-2027-changes).

## Is IGCSE Business 0264 the same as O Level Business 7081?

Yes.

| 2027 to 2029 | 0264 and 7081 (identical) |
|---|---|
| Paper 1 Short Answer and Data Response | Four questions, 80 marks, 1 hour 30 minutes, 50% |
| Paper 2 Case Study | Four questions on a case study insert, 80 marks, 1 hour 30 minutes, 50% |

The subject content pages are identical, including the formulas and ratios. The 7081 syllabus says it shares content with other Business syllabuses.

Both new codes bring the same changes. Each Paper 1 question has four parts instead of five. Every part (a) question in Paper 2 is now applied to the case study. Application, using what you know about the business in the question, carries more weight. I explain it in my [plain guide to the Business changes](/blog/igcse-business-0264-o-level-7081-changes-2027-parents).

## Are O Levels ending?

Not everywhere, and not for every subject. Posts saying "O Levels are ending" mix up two facts.

**O Level is only offered in some zones.** All three O Level syllabuses say they are not available in all administrative zones. The 7707 and 2281 documents add that O Levels are available to centres in zones 3, 4 and 5.

**Accounting and Economics narrow further from 2028.** This comes from Cambridge's syllabus pages for 7707 and 2281. Each is available in zones 3, 4 and 5 in 2026 and 2027, and from 2028 in zone 4 only. Schools in zones 3 and 5 were told in July 2026. Cambridge suggests they may wish to consider IGCSE Accounting 0452 and IGCSE Economics 0455.

That is all my sources say. The 7081 syllabus was updated in August 2026 and notes that its availability information changed, but it lists no zones. So I make no claim about O Level Business. And nothing here says O Level is ending in zone 4.

What follows is my advice, not a Cambridge statement. If your school is in zone 3 or 5 and you sit Accounting or Economics in 2028 or later, plan for IGCSE. Your exams officer can confirm. Your preparation stays the same, because the content and papers are the same.

Most families get stuck here, because nobody has told them which zone their school is in.

## Which one am I sitting: IGCSE or O Level?

Do not guess from your country or from what friends at another school sit. Check the code. It is written down in four places.

1. **Your Statement of Entry.** This document lists your subjects, papers, dates and venue before the exams. 0452, 0455 or 0264 means IGCSE. 7707, 2281 or 7081 means O Level.
2. **Your school's exams officer.** The syllabuses say exams officers submit the entries. Ask which code you are entered under and which administrative zone the school is in.
3. **The British Council, for private candidates in Pakistan.** You enter through its School Registration System (SRS), not through a school. Check the code there before you pay. I walk through it in my guide to [private candidate registration in Pakistan for 2027](/blog/private-candidate-pakistan-2027-registration).
4. **Cambridge's zone page.** Each syllabus points to cambridgeinternational.org/adminzone to find your administrative zone, and to the O Level syllabus page to check availability.

### Is there a shortcut for students in India?

Yes. The IGCSE syllabuses for 0452, 0455 and 0264 offer a March series for schools in India. The O Level syllabuses list June and November only. So a March entry in one of these subjects should be an IGCSE.

### What about students in Pakistan and the Gulf?

I cannot tell you which zone your school is in, and I will not guess. The syllabuses do not list countries by zone. Use the four checks above. If you sit Accounting or Economics in 2028 or later, ask whether the O Level code will still be available to you.

## Can you sit IGCSE and O Level in the same exam series?

Not in the same subject. Each syllabus lets you combine it with other Cambridge syllabuses in one series, with three exceptions:

- the sister syllabus at the other level (0452 and 7707, 0455 and 2281, 0264 and 7081)
- the IGCSE (9-1) version of the subject (0985, 0987 and 0774)
- any syllabus with the same title at the same level

So you cannot sit IGCSE Accounting 0452 and O Level Accounting 7707 in June 2027. Pick one.

The rule is per subject. Nothing in these syllabuses stops you sitting IGCSE Accounting and O Level Economics together, if both are available where you sit.

<Tip title="One code per subject">
Before you pay any exam fee, check that each subject appears once, under one code. Two codes for the same subject in one series is a mistake to fix straight away.
</Tip>

## What grades can you get in IGCSE and O Level?

Both start at A\*. The difference is at the bottom.

| | IGCSE | O Level |
|---|---|---|
| Grades on the certificate | A\*, A, B, C, D, E, F, G | A\*, A, B, C, D, E |
| Lowest grade | G | E |
| Below the lowest grade | Ungraded | Ungraded |

So O Level has no F or G. Work below the grade E standard is ungraded, and an ungraded result shows on the statement of results but not on the certificate.

Both sets of syllabuses say students with A\* to C are well prepared for Cambridge International AS & A Level.

## Do universities treat IGCSE and O Level the same?

I can only report what the syllabuses say.

- Both sets say many universities ask for Cambridge AS & A Levels together with IGCSEs or O Levels, or equivalent.
- The IGCSE syllabuses add a line about UK ENIC, the UK agency that compares international qualifications. It benchmarked Cambridge IGCSE and found it comparable to the UK GCSE. The O Level syllabuses do not include that paragraph.

The O Level documents simply do not mention that study. They say nothing against O Level. My advice: each university sets its own entry rules, so if one names a qualification or a minimum grade, follow it. If you are unsure, email the admissions office before you choose.

## Does it change how I should study?

Very little. For each pair, the content, the papers and the assessment objective weightings are the same. Three practical points, from me as a teacher:

- **Use one checklist.** Both codes share the 2027 to 2029 topic list. My [IGCSE Accounting 0452 syllabus checklist](/syllabus/igcse-accounting-0452) works for 7707 as well.
- **Practise from the sister code's past papers too.** The [IGCSE Economics 0455 past papers](/past-papers/igcse-economics-0455) cover the same subject as 2281, for example. Papers before 2027 follow the old syllabus, so check each question's topic against your checklist.
- **Know your grade scale.** IGCSE reports grades F and G. O Level stops at E. Your revision does not change.

<Tip title="When you switch from O Level to IGCSE">
If your school moves you from 7707 to 0452, or from 2281 to 0455, keep your notes. The topic lists match word for word. Change the code on your folders and check your new exam dates.
</Tip>

## FAQ

### Is IGCSE harder than O Level?

No source says so. Cambridge places them at the same level, and for Accounting, Economics and Business the 2027 papers and content are identical. The IGCSE grade scale is wider, going down to G.

### Is O Level the same as IGCSE?

They are different qualifications from the same board, at the same level. In these three subjects the papers and topics are the same. The codes, grade scales, exam series and zone availability differ.

### Can I take both IGCSE and O Level in the same subject?

Not in the same exam series. Each syllabus lists its sister code at the other level as an exception to the combining rule.

### Are O Levels being stopped in Pakistan?

My sources do not say that. Cambridge says O Level Accounting 7707 and O Level Economics 2281 will be available in administrative zone 4 only from 2028. Ask your exams officer, or the British Council if you are a private candidate, which zone and codes apply to you.

### Does IGCSE have a March exam?

Yes, for schools in India. The 0452, 0455 and 0264 syllabuses offer a March series there. The O Level syllabuses list June and November only.

### Do universities prefer IGCSE or O Level?

The syllabuses do not rank them. Cambridge says they are at the same level. Both sets of syllabuses say many universities ask for IGCSEs or O Levels, or equivalent, alongside AS & A Levels. Check with each admissions office.
