Check your exam date first. Cambridge is replacing O Level Business Studies 7115 with **O Level Business 7081**, first assessed in **June 2027**. Sitting in 2026, you take 7115. Sitting in June 2027 or later, you take 7081. The way your answers are marked carries over almost entirely, so what follows applies to both.

## The 7115 papers (exams in 2026)

| | Paper 1 | Paper 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Name | Short Answer and Data Response | Case Study |
| Time | 1 hour 30 minutes | 1 hour 30 minutes |
| Series | June and November | June and November |

Both papers test the same four skills, in different amounts:

| Skill (assessment objective) | Whole qualification | Paper 1 | Paper 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| AO1 Knowledge and understanding | 40% | 50% | 30% |
| AO2 Application | 20% | 20% | 20% |
| AO3 Analysis | 25% | 20% | 30% |
| AO4 Evaluation | 15% | 10% | 20% |

Half of Paper 1 is knowledge, so a student who has learned the content well can do well there. Paper 2 gives half its marks to analysis and evaluation. Students who know the content but cannot build an argument usually see a gap between their two papers, and this table is the reason.

## One point, through all four skills

A single point about a café, built up one skill at a time:

| Skill | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| Knowledge | Market share is a firm's sales as a percentage of total sales in its market. |
| Application | The café's market share fell when a chain opened on the same street. |
| Analysis | It now sells fewer coffees a day, so revenue falls, so the monthly loan repayment takes a bigger share of what comes in. |
| Evaluation | The loan is the more urgent problem, so cutting costs matters more this year than winning back customers. |

A Paper 1 part (a) might only want the first line. A Paper 2 recommendation needs all four.

## What the examiners rewarded in June 2024

From the Cambridge examiner report for 7115:

- **Precise definitions.** Close is not enough. Learn key terms word for word.
- **Application and analysis for each separate point.** The same point made twice is credited once. Two different points, each applied and analysed, beat one point written three ways.
- **Evaluation means explaining why one factor matters more than another.** Weaker answers repeated earlier points or gave a decision with no support.
- **Answers must fit the case.** Generic answers, and answers built on misreading the case study, scored poorly.

<Tip>
Part (e) of each Paper 1 question carries no application marks. Copying details from the question into it earns nothing there. Spend the time on the chain of analysis and a clear judgement at the end.
</Tip>

The long recommendation questions follow the same plan as in IGCSE Business, which I set out in my [article on 0450 justify questions](/blog/igcse-business-0450-justify-evaluate-questions). The two syllabuses share the same four skills and the same weightings.

## Sitting in June 2027? What changes with 7081

7081 keeps the same shape: a short answer and data response paper and a case study paper. In the specimen papers, both are out of 80 marks, and the Paper 2 case study is 1 hour 30 minutes with the case in a separate insert. Grades run from A* to E.

The change is in the weighting:

| Skill | 7115 | 7081 |
|---|---|---|
| AO1 Knowledge and understanding | 40% | 35% |
| AO2 Application | 20% | 30% |
| AO3 Analysis | 25% | 22% |
| AO4 Evaluation | 15% | 13% |

Application rises from 20% to 30%. Cambridge says the objectives were reweighted to put more emphasis on context and critical thinking. Fewer marks now come from knowing a definition, and more from using it for the business in the question. Tying every point to the case was always good technique. Under 7081 it is worth half as much again.

## A plan to June 2027 (7081)

| Months | Focus |
|---|---|
| November to January | Finish the content. For every topic, learn the key definitions word for word. |
| February | Paper 1 questions by topic, marked strictly. Check every answer has a fact from the question in it. |
| March to April | Full Paper 2 case studies, timed at 1 hour 30 minutes. Rewrite your weakest recommendation each week. |
| May | Full mock papers. Use the 7081 specimen papers as well as 7115 past papers, because 7081 has none of its own yet. |
