Before anything else, check your exam date. Cambridge is replacing O Level Business Studies 7115 with **O Level Business 7081**, first assessed in **June 2027**. If you sit in 2026, you take 7115. If you sit in June 2027 or later, you take 7081. Most of what examiners reward carries over, so this article covers both.

## The 7115 papers (exams in 2026)

| | Paper 1 | Paper 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Name | Short Answer and Data Response | Case Study |
| Time | 1 hour 30 minutes | 1 hour 30 minutes |
| Series | June and November | June and November |

You take both papers. The skills each paper rewards are different, and this is the table to learn:

| Skill (assessment objective) | Whole qualification | Paper 1 | Paper 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| AO1 Knowledge and understanding | 40% | 50% | 30% |
| AO2 Application | 20% | 20% | 20% |
| AO3 Analysis | 25% | 20% | 30% |
| AO4 Evaluation | 15% | 10% | 20% |

Paper 1 leans on knowing your terms precisely. Paper 2 leans on analysis and judgement. A student who knows the content well but cannot build an argument will do noticeably better on Paper 1 than Paper 2, and the table shows why.

## The four skills, in plain words

- **Knowledge (AO1):** the correct definition or idea. "Market share is a firm's sales as a percentage of total sales in the market."
- **Application (AO2):** using it for this business. "The café's market share fell because a new chain opened on the same street."
- **Analysis (AO3):** following the effect through. "So it sells fewer coffees each day, which lowers revenue, which makes the loan repayments harder to meet."
- **Evaluation (AO4):** weighing it up and deciding. "The loan is the bigger risk, so cutting costs matters more right now than winning back customers."

## What examiners reward (from the June 2024 report)

The Cambridge examiner report for 7115 in June 2024 gives clear signals:

- **Precise definitions.** Close enough is not enough. Learn the key terms word for word.
- **Separate application and analysis for each point.** If you make the same point twice, it is credited once. Two different points, each applied and analysed, beat one point written three ways.
- **Part (e) of the Paper 1 questions has no application marks.** The marks there go to knowledge, analysis and evaluation, so spend your time on the argument and the judgement.
- **Evaluation means explaining why one factor matters more than another.** Weaker answers repeated earlier points or gave a decision with no support.
- **Answers must fit the case.** Generic answers, or answers built on a misreading of the case study, scored poorly.

<Tip>
In Paper 1, part (e) of each question carries no application marks. Do not spend time copying details from the question into it. Put the time into the chain of analysis and a clear judgement at the end.
</Tip>

## Sitting in June 2027? What changes with 7081

O Level Business 7081 keeps the same shape: two papers, a short answer and data response paper and a case study paper. In the specimen papers, both are out of 80 marks, and the Paper 2 case study is 1 hour 30 minutes with the case in a separate insert. Grades run from A* to E.

The big change is the weighting of the four skills:

| Skill | 7115 (whole qualification) | 7081 (whole qualification) |
|---|---|---|
| AO1 Knowledge and understanding | 40% | 35% |
| AO2 Application | 20% | 30% |
| AO3 Analysis | 25% | 22% |
| AO4 Evaluation | 15% | 13% |

Application goes from 20% to 30%. Cambridge says the objectives were reweighted to put more emphasis on context and critical thinking. In practice, that means fewer marks for knowing a definition and more for using it in the business in front of you. Tying every point to the business in the question was always good technique. Under 7081 it is worth half as many marks again.

## How to use this

1. Find out which code you are entered for: 7115 or 7081.
2. Learn the key definitions word for word. They are the fastest marks on Paper 1.
3. Time yourself on Paper 2: 1 hour 30 minutes on one case study, start to finish.
4. If you are on 7081, practise with the 7081 specimen papers as well as 7115 past papers, since 7081 has no past papers yet.
