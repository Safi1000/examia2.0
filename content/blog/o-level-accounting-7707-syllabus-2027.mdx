Yes, O Level Accounting 7707 changes for 2027. The good news first: Paper 2 is untouched, so almost everything you practise still counts. The changes are a longer multiple choice paper, two new short topics, and an updated list of ratios. Here is each one, taken from Cambridge's 2027 to 2029 syllabus and compared with the 2026 version.

## Who the new syllabus affects

Cambridge says the 7707 syllabus "has been reviewed and revised for first examination in 2027". It covers exams in 2027, 2028 and 2029, in the June and November series.

- **Sitting in June or November 2027 or later**, including resits: you are on the new syllabus. Use the syllabus for the year you sit the exam, not the year you started.
- **Sitting in November 2026:** you are still on the 2026 syllabus.

Availability is changing too. On the 7707 syllabus page, Cambridge says the syllabus is available in administrative zones 3, 4 and 5 in 2026 and 2027, and **from 2028 in zone 4 only**. Schools in zones 3 and 5 were told in July 2026, and Cambridge suggests they may wish to consider IGCSE Accounting 0452. If you plan to sit in 2028 or later, ask your exams officer which zone your school is in.

The two syllabuses are close cousins. Cambridge says 7707 shares content with other accounting syllabuses, and that textbooks endorsed for IGCSE Accounting are suitable for O Level.

## The two papers

| | 2026 syllabus | 2027 to 2029 syllabus |
|---|---|---|
| **Paper 1** Multiple Choice | 35 questions, 35 marks, 1 hour 15 minutes | 40 questions, 40 marks, 1 hour 30 minutes |
| Paper 1 weighting | 30% | 30% |
| **Paper 2** Structured Written Paper | 100 marks, 1 hour 45 minutes, five compulsory questions of 20 marks | No change |
| Paper 2 weighting | 70% | 70% |

Calculators may be used on both papers. Grades are A\* to E.

### What this means in practice

- **Paper 1 gives you slightly more time per question:** about 2 minutes 9 seconds before, 2 minutes 15 seconds now. The syllabus says directly that some questions require calculations, so the extra time is useful.
- **Each multiple choice mark is worth a little less:** 30% spread over 35 marks was about 0.86% of your grade per mark. Over 40 marks it is 0.75%.
- **Paper 2 is exactly as before.** Five compulsory questions worth 20 marks each, in 105 minutes. That is 21 minutes per question, or about a minute per mark.

The syllabus lists the kinds of task Paper 2 sets: calculations, completing a cash book, completing an incomplete statement of accounts, preparing accounts from information in the question, and preparing suspense accounts and journal entries. Every question starts from stimulus material, and Cambridge tells you to use the marks for each part as a guide to how much to write.

## The full topic list for 2027

| Topic | What it covers |
|---|---|
| 1 The fundamentals of accounting | Purpose of accounting, the accounting equation |
| 2 Sources and recording of data | Double entry, business documents, books of prime entry |
| 3 Verification of accounting records | Trial balance, correction of errors, bank reconciliation, control accounts |
| 4 Accounting procedures | Capital and revenue items, depreciation and disposal, other payables and receivables, irrecoverable debts and the allowance for them, inventory valuation |
| 5 Preparation of financial statements | Sole traders, partnerships, limited companies, manufacturing accounts, clubs and societies, incomplete records |
| 6 Analysis and interpretation | Ratios, interpreting ratios, inter-business comparison, interested parties, limitations of accounting statements |
| 7 Accounting concepts and modern practice | Accounting concepts, ethical considerations, technology and sustainability |

Topics 1 to 6 have been reworded and reordered in places, but the core of the course is the same.

## What changed in the content

### New: 7.2 Ethical considerations

You need:

- the need for an ethical framework in accounting
- the five fundamental principles: integrity, objectivity, professional competence and due care, confidentiality, and professional behaviour
- why applying an ethical framework matters to stakeholders and to society

### New: 7.3 Technology and sustainability

You need:

- the use of digital applications for all or part of the accounting records
- the need to store accounting data safely and sustainably, and the risks of not doing so
- the types of storage system: manual, data storage devices, cloud services and other digital services
- the advantages and disadvantages of each type

The syllabus adds that you do not need to know any specific accounting software.

### Topic 7 has been reshaped

In 2026, Topic 7 was "Accounting principles and policies". It covered ten principles plus a section on accounting policies: the influence of international accounting standards, and the objectives of comparability, relevance, reliability and understandability.

From 2027, Topic 7 is "Accounting concepts and modern practice". The ten concepts stay (business entity, consistency, duality, going concern, historic cost, matching or accruals, materiality, money measurement, prudence and realisation). The accounting policies section is gone, and Cambridge confirms the international standards list has been removed. Ethics and technology take its place.

### The ratio list has been updated

From 2027 you must know ten ratios:

| Group | Ratios |
|---|---|
| Profitability | Gross profit margin, mark-up, profit margin, return on capital employed (ROCE) |
| Liquidity | Current (working capital) ratio, acid test (liquid) ratio |
| Efficiency | Rate of inventory turnover (times), inventory turnover (days), trade receivables turnover (days), trade payables turnover (days) |

Compared with 2026, inventory turnover in days is new, "gross margin" is now "gross profit margin", and ROCE is now written as profit for the year before interest, divided by capital employed, times 100. Capital employed is issued shares plus reserves plus non-current liabilities.

The syllabus is strict here: you must use the formulas given in the syllabus, and "these are the only formulas accepted". Learn them exactly as printed.

### A longer list of interested parties

Topic 6.4 now names owners, managers, employees, banks, investors and lenders, suppliers, customers, governments and tax authorities, club members, and other parties such as public and environmental bodies. The 2026 list was shorter, so add employees, suppliers and customers to your notes.

### Smaller changes

The aims, the command words and the wording of the assessment objectives have been updated. An introduction has been added to each topic. The assessment objective weightings for the whole qualification were rounded differently, moving from 65%, 25% and 10% to 66%, 24% and 10%. Cambridge says the reworded objectives test the same knowledge and skills.

## What examiners reward

The 2027 weightings tell you where the marks are:

| Assessment objective | Whole qualification | Paper 1 | Paper 2 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| AO1 Knowledge and understanding | 66% | 80% | 60% |
| AO2 Analysis | 24% | 20% | 25% |
| AO3 Evaluation | 10% | 0% | 15% |

Two thirds of the qualification rewards knowing the method and applying it correctly. In practice that means the right format, the right headings, figures on the right side, and arithmetic you can show. Neat layout is not decoration in Accounting: it is how the examiner finds your marks.

The 15% of Paper 2 for evaluation rewards you for interpreting figures and drawing conclusions. Look at the command words. "Advise" means write down a course of action. "Justify" means support it with evidence. "Evaluate" means judge the value or importance of something. In each case, the examiner wants a decision supported by the figures in the question, not a list of general points. Topic 6.5 also gives you non-financial aspects to mention, such as the skill of the workforce, the location of the business and the economic climate.

<Tip>
For every ratio, write three lines: the formula, the figures substituted in, and the answer with its unit. Percentages for margins, mark-up and ROCE; a ratio such as 1.8 : 1 for the current and acid test ratios; times or days for the turnover ratios. Writing the formula first also stops you mixing up ratios that look alike, such as gross profit margin and mark-up.
</Tip>

## What to do about it

1. **Use the 2027 to 2029 syllabus as your checklist.** Tick off each subtopic as you finish it.
2. **Learn 7.2 and 7.3 as short lists**, each point with a one-line example of your own. Both topics are compact, so they are quick marks once learned.
3. **Rewrite your ratio sheet** to match the ten ratios and formulas above, word for word.
4. **Keep doing past papers.** Paper 2 has not changed, so every past Paper 2 is still exactly the right practice. For Paper 1, practise in sets of 40 questions in 90 minutes.
5. **Master the verification topics.** The syllabus names suspense accounts and journal entries as Paper 2 tasks, and both depend on a solid trial balance. My [trial balance worked example](/blog/igcse-accounting-trial-balance) is a good place to start.
