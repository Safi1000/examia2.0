If your child is studying Business for IGCSE or O Level, you may have seen two new codes on a school letter or a book list: 0264 and 7081. You may be wondering whether this means a new subject, new books or a harder exam. The short answer is that it is the same subject with a new name and code, a few new topics, and one important change in how marks are given. Here is what that means in plain terms.

## The short answer

| | Up to 2026 | From 2027 |
|---|---|---|
| IGCSE | Business Studies, code 0450 | Business, code **0264** |
| O Level | Business Studies, code 7115 | Business, code **7081** |

Cambridge's own syllabus documents say the name has changed (from "Business Studies" to "Business"), there is a new code, and the content and marking have been revised. The new syllabuses cover exams in 2027, 2028 and 2029.

**Who does this affect?** Any student who sits IGCSE or O Level Business in 2027 or later. A student sitting in 2026 takes 0450 or 7115 as before.

## What stays the same

Most of what your child is used to does not change.

| | 0264 and 7081 |
|---|---|
| Number of papers | Two, and every student sits both |
| Paper 1 | Short answer and data response: 1 hour 30 minutes, 80 marks, 50% of the grade |
| Paper 2 | Case study, with the case printed in a separate insert: 1 hour 30 minutes, 80 marks, 50% of the grade |
| Coursework | None. Both papers are written exams |
| Grades | IGCSE 0264: A\* to G. O Level 7081: A\* to E |
| Exam sessions | June and November (IGCSE 0264 is also offered in March for schools in India) |
| Private candidates | Can enter for both |

The four skills that are marked also stay the same: knowledge, application, analysis and evaluation. Cambridge says the reworded objectives "test the same knowledge and skills as in the previous syllabus."

## What changes

### 1. More marks for applying knowledge to the business

This is the change that matters most. Cambridge has moved marks towards **application**: using what you know about the particular business in the question, not just reciting a definition.

| Skill | 0450 and 7115 | 0264 and 7081 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Knowledge and understanding | 40% | 35% |
| Application | 20% | 30% |
| Analysis | 25% | 22% |
| Evaluation | 15% | 13% |

What does that look like in practice? Suppose a question is about a small bakery that wants to grow.

- **A knowledge answer:** "Market research is the process of gathering information about customers."
- **An applied answer:** "The bakery only sells in one town, so asking its existing customers which new products they would buy is cheap and quick."

Both are correct, but from 2027 the second kind of sentence earns a larger share of the marks. Students who learn their notes well but write general answers will feel this change the most.

### 2. Changes to the question papers

Cambridge lists two changes to the papers themselves:

- **Paper 1:** each question now has four parts instead of five, and the marks are split differently between them.
- **Paper 2:** all the part (a) questions are now applied questions, so even the first, shorter questions on the case study expect your child to refer to the business.

### 3. New and reorganised topics

The course is organised into six main areas: business activity, people, marketing, operations, finance, and outside influences such as the economy. Within them:

- **Two new topics:** how technology is changing production (for example automation, 3D printing and contactless payments) and sustainable production (for example renewable energy, less waste, recycling and environmentally friendly packaging).
- **Digital methods of communication** have been added to the people in business topic.
- **A list of formulas and ratios** is now printed in the syllabus, including market share, labour productivity, break even output, profit margin, return on capital employed and the current and acid test ratios.
- **Some content has been removed and some reorganised.** Cambridge does not list every removed item in its summary of changes, so the syllabus itself is the place to check.

## Three practical questions parents ask

### Will my child's books and notes still work?

Mostly, yes. The bulk of the content is the same. Cambridge says textbooks endorsed for exams from 2027 are suitable for the new syllabuses. If you are buying a new book, check that the cover or listing says it is for exams from 2027. Older notes are still useful, but they will not cover the two new topics or the new formula list.

### Are there past papers for the new codes?

Not yet, because the first exams are in 2027. Cambridge has published specimen papers, which show the new question styles. Older 0450 and 7115 past papers are still good practice, because the skills are the same. Just remember that Paper 1 questions in old papers have five parts, not four, and that the new papers will reward application more.

### Can my child take both IGCSE and O Level Business?

No, not in the same exam session. Both syllabus documents say a candidate cannot combine 0264 with 7081 in a single exam series. Pick one.

## What grade boundaries will look like

Nobody knows the grade thresholds for 0264 or 7081 yet. Cambridge sets them after each session's papers are marked. For a sense of the current system, in June 2026 an A\* in 0450 and 7115 (variant 1) needed 95 out of 160, about 59%. That number came from the old papers and the old mark split, so treat it as background, not a target for 2027. The [grade thresholds](/grade-thresholds) page shows recent sessions for [IGCSE Business 0450](/grade-thresholds/igcse-business-0450) and [O Level Business 7115](/grade-thresholds/o-level-business-7115).

## What I would check with the school

- **Which code your child is entered for.** It should be 0264 (IGCSE) or 7081 (O Level) for any Business exam in 2027 or later.
- **Whether the two new topics have been taught.** Technology in production and sustainable production are easy to miss if teaching started from older notes.
- **Whether practice includes the specimen papers**, not only old past papers.
- **How written answers are marked.** Ask whether the teacher marks for application to the business in the question, since that is now 30% of the marks.

If your child is a private candidate, there is no school to check the entry code, so look at it carefully in the British Council registration system. I explain the steps in [how private candidates register in Pakistan for 2027](/blog/private-candidate-pakistan-2027-registration).

## How to help at home

You do not need to know Business to help. After your child answers a practice question, ask one thing: **"Where in your answer did you mention the business in the question?"** If the answer is "nowhere", the response could have been written about any company, and under the new syllabus it will lose marks for that. This one question is the most useful habit for 0264 and 7081.
